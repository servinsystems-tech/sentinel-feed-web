A trial is included only when explicitly offered in your Paddle checkout. Review the trial duration, first charge and cancellation terms displayed there before confirming.

Monthly subscriptions

Subscriptions renew automatically each month. If you contact us within 7 days of a charge and have not meaningfully used the Service during that billing period (i.e., have not received or accessed more than one daily briefing), we will issue a full refund for that charge at our discretion.
