04 / BEFORE YOU SUBSCRIBE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

No inflated promises. Here is how the service works.

When will I receive my briefing?

The pipeline is scheduled on business days, Monday through Friday. New subscriptions are picked up by the scheduled synchronization; access is not provisioned instantly at checkout. Delivery depends on source availability and successful processing.

Does SentinelFeed monitor my infrastructure?

No. SentinelFeed analyzes public vulnerability disclosures. It does not scan your systems or determine your actual exposure. Your team verifies applicability and remediation against official advisories.

Is the analysis reviewed by a human?

The service uses automated, AI-assisted analysis. It can contain mistakes or omissions. Source links help you verify the information before acting; it does not replace your vulnerability management program.

How does Enterprise delivery work?

Enterprise includes the audio briefing and a structured JSON payload sent to a configured HTTPS endpoint. You supply the endpoint and implement any downstream integration. It is not a ready-made Slack or SIEM connector.

Is a free trial included?

This page does not enroll you in a self-service free trial. Any trial or introductory terms must be explicitly shown in your Paddle checkout before you confirm a subscription.

Who handles payments and cancellations?

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for checkout, billing and invoices. You can manage billing through the links in your Paddle receipt. Cancelling stops future renewals; refund eligibility is described in the Refund Policy.
