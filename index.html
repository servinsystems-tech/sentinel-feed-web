In this example, an internet-facing gateway has a critical vulnerability with confirmed exploitation. That exploitation evidence makes the finding deserve attention ahead of severity alone.

If your organization used this gateway, the next step would be to check whether an affected version was deployed. Review the official vendor advisory, then prioritize the recommended update through your normal change controls.

The point is to connect the finding to a decision: what might be exposed, why it matters, and what your team should verify next. A severity number provides context, but it does not establish your organization's actual exposure.

04 / BEFORE YOU SUBSCRIBE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

No inflated promises. Here is how the service works.

When will I receive my briefing?

The pipeline is scheduled on business days, Monday through Friday. New subscriptions are picked up by the scheduled synchronization; access is not provisioned instantly at checkout. Delivery depends on source availability and successful processing.

Does SentinelFeed monitor my infrastructure?

No. SentinelFeed analyzes public vulnerability disclosures. It does not scan your systems or determine your actual exposure. Your team verifies applicability and remediation against official advisories.

Is the analysis reviewed by a human?

The service uses automated, AI-assisted analysis. It can contain mistakes or omissions. Source links help you verify the information before acting; it does not replace your vulnerability management program.

How does Enterprise delivery work?

Enter your public HTTPS endpoint before checkout. After Paddle confirms your subscription at the next scheduled sync, the buyer receives the audio briefing and the structured JSON is delivered to that endpoint. You implement any downstream integration; it is not a ready-made Slack or SIEM connector.

Only Individual includes a 7-day free trial, then $99/month unless cancelled before the trial ends. Teams ($349/month) and Enterprise ($999/month) have no free trial. Paddle Checkout displays the definitive trial, payment-method and billing terms before you confirm.

Who handles payments and cancellations?

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for checkout, billing and invoices. You can manage billing through the links in your Paddle receipt. Cancelling stops future renewals; refund eligibility is described in the Refund Policy.
